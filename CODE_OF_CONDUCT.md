# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances,
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
- public or private harassment,
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission,
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and other channels) and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **tech@radianzz.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they deem inappropriate, following the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
